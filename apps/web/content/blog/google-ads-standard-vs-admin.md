Updated October 10, 2026

Google Ads “Access and security” is where agencies lose a day. The client sees Admin, Standard, Read only, and email payments. They pick Admin because it sounds like “the agency can work,” or they pick Read only because Admin sounds dangerous.

## Standard is the campaign grant

You can build, edit, pause, and report. You cannot add users or change billing. If that matches the SOW, write **Standard** in the request. Do not write “full access.”

## Admin is account control

Users, billing, and settings. Need it when you operate the MCC relationship, fix billing profiles, or the client has no one internally who will click those screens. It is not a shortcut for “so we do not have to ask again.”

## MCC is not a permission

A manager account links client accounts. Each client account still has its own users and roles. Getting linked via MCC with Standard on that account is enough for media. The [Google Ads guide](/guides/google-ads-access) covers the click path.

## Email payments and Read only

Read only is reporting. Email payments is a billing inbox, not campaign access. Neither ships ads.

Use the [access-level tool](/tools/access-level) (Google + “run and edit campaigns” → Standard) if you want a page the client can open without logging into AuthHub.
