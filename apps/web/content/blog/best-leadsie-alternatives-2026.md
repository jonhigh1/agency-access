## Shared job: one branded link → client grants access

Every serious tool here solves the same job: replace password-chasing and screenshot PDFs with a **client-facing authorization link** that uses each platform's official permission flow. Access lives **on the platforms**. No vendor "owns" the grant; clients can revoke it there.

None of these products invented the category alone. Decide on what happens **after** the first grant: how you pay when volume spikes, how deep branding and intake go, how broad the connectors are, whether tokens stay healthy, and how API / automation fit your stack.

## Why agencies look for Leadsie alternatives in 2026

Leadsie remains the mature, credit-based baseline many agencies already trust. Teams still shortlist alternatives when:

1. **Credits and overages** — Busy months mean **$50** overage packs on top of **$59 / $129 / $299** plans (3 / 10 / 50 onboarding credits).
2. **Branding depth** — Peers pitch deeper onboarding-page customization (copy, GIF, white-label paths).
3. **Shopify Partner path** — ClientInvite and others advertise Shopify Partner / collaborator flows Leadsie's public materials do not emphasize the same way (attribute peer claim; verify for your use case).
4. **Token reconnect pain** — Expired or revoked OAuth access stalls campaigns; buyers look for refresh, health monitoring, and audit packaging. See [OAuth token management for agencies](/blog/oauth-token-management-agencies) for the ops side.
5. **Intake in the same link** — Wanting questionnaires and OAuth in one client flow (AuthHub and AgencyAccess both offer this—**parity, not an exclusive**).

Keep the stay-reasons honest: Leadsie's **platform breadth**, **audit / view-only credit pools**, **Access Detective / Meta asset helpers**, **GoHighLevel-adjacent maturity**, and **annual credit pooling** are real reasons not to switch.

## Deep cards

### Leadsie (baseline)

**Who it's for:** Agencies that already like credit pooling, need the widest long-tail platform set, run lots of **view-only audits**, or rely on Meta asset creation / Access Detective-style helpers.

**Pricing snapshot (2026-09-25 PT):** Monthly **$59 / $129 / $299** for **3 / 10 / 50** onboarding credits (audit pools **10 / 50 / 250**). Overage packs **$50**. Annual display roughly **$49 / $107** (and Pro yearly on-page). 14-day trial; unused credits roll (~3 months per their materials).

**Strengths:** Mature category leader; broad platforms; separate audit metering; multi-brand on Pro; large install base.

**Trade-offs:** Busy months = overage math; branding depth and Shopify Partner path are where peers attack; token refresh is not the primary product story.

**Pick Leadsie if…** credits + audits + breadth still match how you sell. See also [Leadsie pricing teardown](/compare/leadsie-pricing).

### ClientInvite

**Who it's for:** Agencies that want the simplest **flat-fee** story and mainly need Meta, Google, Shopify, and LinkedIn.

**Pricing snapshot:** **$29**/mo entry; **$89**/mo Agency with **unlimited** connections (vendor claim). 14-day trial. No overage packs on that model.

**Strengths:** Anti-credit clarity; Shopify Partner access pitch; deeper branding narrative vs logo-and-colors-only peers.

**Trade-offs:** Narrower long-tail than Leadsie / AgencyAccess; not the token-vault / Infisical story.

**Pick ClientInvite if…** unlimited flat at **$89** is the decision and your stack fits their four-platform focus.

### AgencyAccess

**Who it's for:** Teams that want **intake + deep branding**, a **30-day** trial, invite-based caps, and a documented **public API** (plus Zapier on Premium+)—not "Zapier-only."

**Pricing snapshot:** Monthly **$44 / $99 / $199** for **5 / 15 / 50** clients/month (Agency: 50 then custom). Annual **$33 / $74 / $149**/mo. Hit the cap → wait for reset or upgrade (no auto overage packs like Leadsie).

**Strengths:** Intake in-flow; branding emphasis; longer trial; broader niche integrations; automation paths via **API + Zapier**.

**Trade-offs:** Caps are real (Premium is **15**/month, not unlimited); token auto-refresh is not their lead story.

**Pick AgencyAccess if…** branding + intake + 30-day evaluation matter most. Full binary: [/compare/agencyaccess-alternative](/compare/agencyaccess-alternative). Three-way: [/compare/leadsie-vs-agencyaccess-vs-authhub](/compare/leadsie-vs-agencyaccess-vs-authhub).

### AuthHub

**Who it's for:** Agencies that care about **automatic OAuth token refresh** (where providers support it), **Infisical**-backed token references + **audit logs**, **intake in the same authorization link**, and predictable **active-client** tiers—without pretending to cover every Leadsie niche.

**Pricing snapshot:** Monthly **Starter $29 / Growth $79 / Scale $149** for **5 / 20 / 50** active clients. Annual display often **~$24 / $66 / $124**/mo on [/pricing](/pricing). **API + webhooks from Growth up** (not Starter). 14-day trial. **Not** unlimited clients on all plans. **No SOC 2** claim on this page—Infisical + audit only. US-based support; **no** same-day SLA marketed here.

**Strengths:** Token lifecycle + vault/audit packaging; intake-in-link; flat caps that are easy to forecast inside the tier; Growth+ automation.

**Trade-offs:** Honest **15+** connectors—Leadsie, AgencyAccess, and Access Pilot are broader on niches. AuthHub is built for **humans and agents** sharing the same access path—without overclaiming a separate MCP product page.

**Pick AuthHub if…** expired-token reconnects, Infisical/audit, intake-in-link, or Growth API/webhooks are the bottleneck. Full switcher math: [/compare/leadsie-alternative](/compare/leadsie-alternative).

### ClientFuse

**Who it's for:** Agencies that want lower published list tiers, **access diagnostics**, and are evaluating vendor **AI / ad-account chat** marketing—while accepting a **narrower** platform set than Leadsie.

**Pricing snapshot:** **$39 / $79 / $199** for **5 / 20 / 75** clients/mo (Enterprise custom). 14-day trial. Webhooks/Zapier listed as coming soon on their compare page.

**Strengths:** Price/capacity pitch vs Leadsie; diagnostics; AI features as they market them.

**Trade-offs:** They state Leadsie wins on TikTok / Snapchat / Pinterest-class breadth; treat AI claims as **their** story, not AuthHub's.

**Pick ClientFuse if…** diagnostics + **$79**/20 clients is the gate and Meta/Google/LinkedIn cover your book.

### OnboardClient

**Who it's for:** Teams that want to **test one-link access at $0** list price.

**Pricing snapshot:** Positions as **100% free / unlimited** invites, sponsored by Reaktion.com. Zapier for CRM updates. Branding and universal website link called out on-site.

**Strengths:** Zero list price; fast experiment; Shopify + ads/analytics focus.

**Trade-offs:** "Forever free" is **their** claim—don't bank ops on unstated economics; not an AuthHub-class token/vault story.

**Pick OnboardClient if…** you need a free experiment before paying any peer.

### Access Pilot

**Who it's for:** Freelancers and agencies that prefer **pay-as-you-go credits** and claim **50+** platform breadth without a monthly subscription.

**Pricing snapshot:** Individual **$1**/credit; Agency **$5**/credit (white-label). **5 free credits** on signup. Credits deduct on successful grant, not on link generation.

**Strengths:** No monthly fee; broad platform marketing; Agency white-label credits.

**Trade-offs:** Per-grant cost rises with volume; not a flat active-client forecast.

**Pick Access Pilot if…** lumpy volume beats a subscription.

### OneClick Onboard (also in market)

**Who it's for:** Agencies primarily on **Google + Meta** who want published flat active-client cards and a free entry tier.

**Pricing snapshot (published cards, 2026-09-25 PT):** Free (1 new client/mo); Growth / Pro / Agency **$29 / $79 / $159** with **15 / 50 / unlimited** active clients on the pricing page cards (annual bill totals shown alongside). Verify monthly vs annual toggle live.

## How to switch without drama

1. **Inventory** active clients, platforms, and which grants still matter.
2. **Dual-run** — keep Leadsie (or your current tool) live while you send the new vendor's link for **new** onboards and **re-authorizations**.
3. **Re-authorize** — no SaaS ports platform permissions for you. Clients must approve again on the new link.
4. **Cut over** only after the clients you care about have completed the new flow and your team trusts status / token health in the new system.

**No** "15-minute migration" or free CS migration promise. Platform access remains until the client or platform revokes it—even if you cancel a vendor subscription.

## FAQ

### What are the best Leadsie alternatives in 2026?

The main Leadsie alternatives agencies shortlist are **ClientInvite** (flat pricing and unlimited connections on its Agency plan), **AgencyAccess** (intake, deep branding, broader integrations, longer trial, public API), **AuthHub** (automatic OAuth token refresh, Infisical-backed storage with audit logs, in-link intake, and **$29 / $79 / $149** active-client tiers), **ClientFuse** (lower published tiers and access diagnostics), and free or credit-based options such as **OnboardClient** and **Access Pilot**—with **OneClick Onboard** also in market for Google/Meta-focused flat cards. The best pick depends on pricing model, platform breadth, and whether token lifecycle and audit matter.

### Credits vs flat vs caps—what's the real difference?

**Leadsie** meters **onboarding credits** (plus audit credits) and sells **$50** overage packs. **ClientInvite** sells a **flat** monthly fee with unlimited connections on Agency. **AgencyAccess** meters **clients/invites per month**. **AuthHub** and **ClientFuse** sell **active-client / clients-per-month tiers**. **Access Pilot** sells **per-grant credits**. **OnboardClient** publishes **$0**. Compare the meter that matches how your agency actually grows—not just the sticker entry price.

### Does AgencyAccess have an API?

**Yes.** AgencyAccess documents a **public REST API** and also offers **Zapier** on higher plans. Do not treat it as Zapier-only. Differentiate peers on **token refresh**, vault/audit packaging, and which automation surfaces you actually need—not on a false "no API" claim.

### Is ClientInvite or AuthHub the better Leadsie alternative?

Choose **ClientInvite** if you want the simplest flat-fee path with unlimited connections at a published **$89** Agency plan and you mainly need Meta, Google, Shopify, and LinkedIn. Choose **AuthHub** if expired-token reconnects are the pain, you need Infisical-style vaulting and audit logs, you want intake in the same authorization link, or you need Growth-tier API and webhooks on predictable **5 / 20 / 50** active-client caps.

### Is free OnboardClient enough?

It can be enough to **prove** one-link onboarding at zero list price. Treat "forever free" as their sponsorship model, not a guaranteed feature roadmap for token health, Infisical-class vaulting, or Growth-tier API. Graduate to a paid peer when those become requirements.

### Should I stay on Leadsie?

**Stay on Leadsie** if you rely on its broader platform set, prospect-audit workflows, Meta/Access Detective helpers, multi-brand Pro features, GoHighLevel-era maturity, or you are happy with credit pooling on annual plans. **Switch** when credits and overages, branding depth, Shopify Partner access, intake-in-link, or automatic token refresh become the bottleneck.
