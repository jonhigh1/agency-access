Updated October 10, 2026

The access email fails when it starts with “please add our BM ID as a partner in Business Settings.” That is true and unreadable.

## What the client needs to hear

1. Which platforms (Facebook/Instagram ads, Google Ads, GA4 — not “the stack”).
2. What you will do there (run campaigns, not pay invoices — unless you will).
3. What they will click (a Facebook login, a Google user invite).
4. That you are not asking for their password.

## A script you can paste

Subject: Access we need to start [campaign / month]

Hi [Name] —

To launch on the accounts we scoped, we need you to grant our agency access — not share a password.

- Meta Ads: Advertiser on the ad account (not billing Admin)
- Google Ads: Standard access on the account we will run
- GA4: Analyst or Editor on the property in the SOW

[One link / three bullets with official steps]

You stay the owner. When the work ends, you revoke us. If a role name on screen does not match this email, stop and ask — do not guess Admin.

Thanks,
[You]

## If they freeze on the screen

Send the matching [platform guide](/guides) or the [access-level tool](/tools/access-level). Do not jump on a screenshare to type their password for them.
