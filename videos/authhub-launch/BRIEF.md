## Intent

A 30-second product-launch showreel. The viewer should feel the craft and understand the product. Jon asked for a dynamic motion-graphics film, "like it's your showreel for a résumé," and said to go all out. Use https://agent-motion.com/ and real AuthHub interface, or both. The confirmed claim: one client link replaces the access chase. AuthHub is the product. Agent Motion is part of the motion language. No voiceover. Type, music, and picture carry the film.

## Assets

- https://agent-motion.com/ — capture for component language, copy, and registry visuals.
- AuthHub product UI — real screens from the app, used as proof inside the reel.
- apps/web/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md — Acid Brutalism v2 is the brand. Coral is the accent. Ink and paper are the grounds.

## Customizations

- No voiceover. On-screen lines carry the script.
- Music bed. Cuts land on the beat. Sound hits on type slams.
- Before/after arc: the access chase, then one link, then the product working.
- Design system: `creative-mode` layout bones, remixed onto AuthHub ink, paper, and coral. Acid yellow appears once. Chosen because the preset's hard borders and square type match Acid Brutalism.

## Notes

- Length is 30 seconds, the top of the requested 15–30 second range, so the reel has room to move.
- Frame is 1920×1080. A résumé showreel is watched wide. The remembered LinkedIn 1080×1350 size was not used.
- Do not invent metrics, customer names, or platform claims the product does not make.
- One coral accent. Acid yellow is one hero moment only.
- Derived mode is autonomous: automation, no storyboard review. Preview or render is still a question after checks.
