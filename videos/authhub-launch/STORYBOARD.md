## Video direction

Palette comes from `frame.md`. Canvas is `cream` (#FAFAFA) or `ink` (#09090B). The accent event is `yellow` (#FF6B35, AuthHub coral). `orange` (#CCFF00) is the single acid hit, and it appears once, on the claim. `pink` is a tint, not a second brand. Type roles: `display-hero` and `display-xl` for the words that are the shot, `mono-kicker` for labels, `body-md` only inside the product surface.

Motion grammar: long-tail settle (`power3`). No bounce, no overshoot, no breathing, no back-half camera drift. There is no spoken voiceover. On-screen lines are the cues. Each piece enters when its line arrives, across the back half of the shot, never dumped in the first quarter. Holds stay still. Subtle jitter is the only aliveness, and only on a resting hero. Internal swaps are velocity-matched cuts.

Rhythm: Frame 1 is the attack. Frame 2 is the held claim. Frame 3 is the product proof and the busiest shot. Frame 4 is a short status punch. Frame 5 holds the lockup still. Energy peaks at the coral slam and at the last letter of the wordmark.

Never: fake ROI numbers, invented customers, purple gradients, glassmorphism, pill-everything, lazy particle fields, infinite marquees, bouncy springs, or a screenshot of Agent Motion standing in for AuthHub. Bottom 17% stays clear of the hero.
